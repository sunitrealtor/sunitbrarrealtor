New Resident Guide

Getting Settled in Placer County

The practical checklist nobody gives you — utilities, California driver's license, vehicle registration, internet setup, schools enrollment, and everything else you need to handle in your first 90 days as a new Placer County resident.

Your first 90 days

What to do, and when
to do it.

California has specific deadlines for some of these requirements. Missing them can result in fines. Here is the order that makes the most practical sense.

Week 1
Set up utilities before or immediately after move-in
Contact SMUD or PG&E for electricity, your city water department for water service, and your internet provider. Schedule these before your move-in date so services are active when you arrive. Gas service through PG&E in most of Placer County — call (800) 743-5000.
Week 2
Update your address everywhere
USPS mail forwarding at usps.com. Update your bank, credit cards, subscriptions, insurance policies, employer HR, and any government agencies. File a change of address with the IRS at irs.gov if relevant.
Week 3–4
Enroll children in school
Contact your school district directly to understand enrollment requirements. You will typically need proof of residency (utility bill or lease), immunization records, and prior school records. Most districts allow online enrollment to start the process.
Day 10–
California driver's license — required within 10 days of establishing residency
California law technically requires a CA license within 10 days of becoming a resident, though enforcement is not immediate. Make a DMV appointment as soon as possible at dmv.ca.gov — walk-in waits can be very long. You will need your current out-of-state license, Social Security card, and two proofs of California residency.
Day 20–
Vehicle registration — required within 20 days
California requires you to register your vehicle within 20 days of establishing residency. You will need a California smog check (required for most vehicles), proof of California insurance, and payment of registration fees. Complete at your local DMV office.
Month 2
Register to vote
Register at registertovote.ca.gov. California offers same-day registration and vote-by-mail as defaults. Placer County Elections Office: 2994 Richardson Dr, Auburn, CA · (530) 886-5650.
Month 3
Establish local healthcare providers
Find a primary care physician, pediatrician, dentist, and any specialists your family needs before you have an urgent need. Sutter Health, Kaiser Permanente, and Adventist Health all have online provider finders for Placer County.
Utilities

Setting up utilities
in Placer County.

Which utility provider serves your home depends on your specific city and address. Here are the main providers across Placer County.

Electricity — most of Placer County
Pacific Gas & Electric (PG&E)
Serves Rocklin, Loomis, Lincoln, and most of unincorporated Placer County. Start service at pge.com or call (800) 743-5000. Have your address and move-in date ready.
pge.com
Electricity — Roseville
Roseville Electric
The City of Roseville operates its own electric utility — one of the advantages of living there. Rates are generally competitive. Start service through the City of Roseville at roseville.ca.us.
roseville.ca.us
Gas — all Placer County
Pacific Gas & Electric (PG&E)
PG&E provides natural gas service throughout Placer County. Set up alongside your electricity service. (800) 743-5000.
pge.com
Water — Roseville
City of Roseville Utilities
Roseville manages its own water and wastewater services. Start service through the city utility billing department at roseville.ca.us or call City Hall.
roseville.ca.us
Water — Rocklin
Rocklin Area Sewer Authority / PCWA
Placer County Water Agency (PCWA) provides water service in much of Rocklin and unincorporated Placer County. pcwa.net · (530) 823-4850.
pcwa.net
Trash & Recycling
Recology / Roseville Utility Billing
Trash service varies by city. Roseville and Rocklin each have city-contracted providers. Contact your city's utility billing office — trash service is usually set up automatically with your address registration.

Internet providers
in Placer County.

Cable / Fiber
Xfinity (Comcast)
Widely available throughout Placer County. Cable and gigabit fiber options. xfinity.com · (800) 934-6489. Often the most available provider in established neighborhoods.
xfinity.com
Fiber
AT&T Fiber
Fiber availability varies by address throughout Placer County. Check att.com for service at your specific address. Gigabit speeds where available. att.com/internet
att.com
Satellite / Backup
Starlink
Excellent option for Loomis, Lincoln, and more rural Placer County addresses where cable infrastructure may be limited. starlink.com. Equipment purchase required upfront.
starlink.com

Libraries & community resources

Placer County Library System.

Placer County Library is free to all county residents. Your library card gives you access to physical books, e-books, audiobooks, digital magazines, research databases, and community programs. Get your card at any branch with proof of Placer County residency.
