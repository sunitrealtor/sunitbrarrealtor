Parks & outdoor life

269 sunny days. Endless things to do.

One of the most compelling reasons families stay in Placer County for decades is the outdoor life. Folsom Lake is at your doorstep. Lake Tahoe is two hours away. And the trails, parks, and recreation options within the county itself are genuinely exceptional.

🏖️
State Recreation Area
Folsom Lake
Folsom Lake Rd, Granite Bay / Folsom · (916) 988-0205 · 75 miles of trails. Boating, swimming, fishing, hiking, horseback riding. Granite Bay Beach is the closest access point for most Placer County residents. Open year-round.
🎢
Adventure Park · Rocklin
Quarry Park Adventures
5373 Pacific St, Rocklin · (916) 824-1680 · quarrypark.com · Zip lines, free-fall jumps, rock climbing, paddle boats in a 160-year-old historic granite quarry. Downtown Rocklin's signature attraction.
🚴
Placer County Trails
American River Parkway
Runs from Sacramento through Placer County to Folsom Lake · 32 continuous miles of paved and unpaved trail. One of the finest urban trail systems in the country. Accessible from multiple points in Placer County.
⛷️
2 Hours Away · I-80 East
Lake Tahoe
Palisades Tahoe, Northstar, Sierra-at-Tahoe for skiing. Crystal clear beaches and hiking in summer. Placer County families make this drive for day trips regularly — not just annual vacations.

Lake Clementine — Auburn
Auburn State Recreation Area · Emerald green lake in the Sierra foothills. Kayaking, swimming, hiking. Far less crowded than Folsom Lake — a genuine local favorite for Placer County residents.

DMV & government services

California requirements
for new residents.

California has specific legal deadlines for driver's license and vehicle registration. Missing them can result in fines. Handle these in the first three weeks after you arrive.

🪪
California Driver's License
Required within 10 days of establishing residency. Make a DMV appointment immediately at dmv.ca.gov — walk-in waits can be 2–4 hours. Bring your out-of-state license, Social Security card, and two proofs of California residency (utility bill, lease, or bank statement).
Roseville DMV: 1699 Orlando Ave · (916) 657-6525
Auburn DMV: 11268 Fuller Dr · Often less crowded
🚗
Vehicle Registration
Required within 20 days of establishing residency. Requires a California smog check (most vehicles over 4 years old), proof of California insurance, and registration fee payment. Smog check stations are widely available throughout Placer County.
Complete at either the Roseville or Auburn DMV office.
🗳️
Other Government Services
Voter registration: registertovote.ca.gov

Placer County Elections: 2994 Richardson Dr, Auburn · (530) 886-5650

Placer County Library card: Free with proof of residency · placer.ca.gov/library

USPS mail forwarding: Set up at usps.com before your move date
