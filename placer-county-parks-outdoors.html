Outdoor Life

Parks, Trails & Outdoor Life in Placer County

269 sunny days a year. Folsom Lake in your backyard. Lake Tahoe two hours away. This is what weekends look like when you live in Placer County — and it is one of the most compelling reasons families stay here for decades.

Signature destinations

The places that make Placer County
feel like permanent vacation.

🏖️
Folsom Lake State Recreation Area
Folsom Lake Rd, Folsom CA 95630 · (916) 988-0205
75 miles of trails surrounding one of Northern California's most beautiful reservoirs. Hiking, biking, boating, jet skiing, fishing, horseback riding, and swimming. Multiple beach areas including Granite Bay Beach — the closest to most Placer County neighborhoods. Year-round access, exceptional on weekday mornings. A defining feature of life here that never gets old.
🎢
Quarry Park Adventures
5373 Pacific St, Rocklin CA 95677 · (916) 824-1680
Located in a 160-year-old granite quarry in the heart of downtown Rocklin — zip lines, free-fall jumps, rock climbing walls, paddle boats, and a kids adventure area. One of the most unique outdoor experiences in the Sacramento region. Popular for birthday parties, corporate team building, and weekend family outings. Open seasonally — check quarrypark.com for hours.
🚴
American River Parkway
Sacramento to Folsom · 32 miles of continuous trail
One of the finest urban trail systems in the country — 32 continuous miles of paved and unpaved trail running from Old Sacramento to Folsom along the American River. Accessible from multiple points throughout Placer County and Sacramento. Ideal for cycling, running, walking, and wildlife watching. Connects to the Folsom Lake trails at its eastern end.
⛷️
Lake Tahoe
2 hours from Rocklin · I-80 east
World-class skiing at Palisades Tahoe, Northstar, and Sierra-at-Tahoe in winter. Crystal-clear beaches, boating, and hiking in summer. The fact that Lake Tahoe is a two-hour drive from most Placer County homes is one of the most undersold quality-of-life features of living here. Families make this drive for day trips regularly — not just annual vacations.
Local parks

City parks, regional parks
& open space across Placer County.

🌳
Mahany Regional Park — Roseville
1545 Pleasant Grove Blvd, Roseville · Baseball, cycling, football, picnic areas, and extensive multi-use sports facilities. One of the largest regional parks in Placer County.
🌳
Johnson-Springview Park — Rocklin
5480 5th St, Rocklin · 46-acre community park with tennis, basketball, volleyball, playgrounds, picnic areas, and the Rruff Off Leash Dog Park adjacent.
🌳
Maidu Regional Park — Roseville
1550 Maidu Dr, Roseville · 50+ acres including the Maidu Museum & Historic Site, sports fields, amphitheater, and extensive picnic areas.
🌳
Miners Ravine Trail — Roseville
Roseville · Popular multi-use trail system running through creekside habitat. Connects multiple neighborhoods and parks throughout western Roseville.
🌳
Twelve Bridges Regional Park — Lincoln
Lincoln · Large community park serving Lincoln's growing population with sports facilities, open space, and community gathering areas.
🌳
Lake Clementine — Auburn
Auburn State Recreation Area · Emerald green lake tucked into the Sierra foothills. Kayaking, swimming, and camping. Less crowded than Folsom Lake — a local favorite.
Dog parks

dog-friendly.

Rruff Off Leash Dog Park — Rocklin
5480 5th St, Rocklin, CA 95677 · rruffhealingheroes.org
Separate areas for small and large dogs. Special needs area for dogs with disabilities. Agility course. Open 6am–10pm.

Saugstad Off Leash Dog Park — Roseville
100 Buljan Dr, Roseville, CA 95747
Fully fenced off-leash area. Separate sections for large and small dogs. Well-maintained with good parking. Open 6am–7pm.

Off-leash dog park with distinct areas for large and small dogs. Ample shade and seating for owners. Open 7am–9pm.

Sports & activities

Youth sports, fitness
& recreation in Placer County.

⚽
AYSO Soccer — Rocklin & Roseville
ayso.org · American Youth Soccer Organization chapters throughout Placer County. Fall and spring seasons. One of the most active youth sports organizations in the region.
⚾
Little League — Rocklin Little League
rocklinlittleleague.com · Spring season baseball and softball for youth ages 4–16. Active program with community involvement and multiple divisions.
🏊
Swim Teams — Placer Swim League
Community swim teams operate through Rocklin, Roseville, Lincoln, and Granite Bay neighborhoods. Summer league for youth swimmers. Highly competitive and well-organized.
🏀
Youth Basketball — Placer County
Multiple recreational basketball programs through Rocklin Parks & Recreation, Roseville Parks & Recreation, and private leagues at local gyms throughout the county.
🎾
Tennis — Maidu Tennis Center
1550 Maidu Dr, Roseville · Multiple courts, lessons, leagues, and clinics for all ages and skill levels. One of the largest public tennis facilities in Placer County.
🎿
Ski & Snowboard — Palisades Tahoe
palisadestahoe.com · 6,000 acres of skiing and snowboarding 2 hours from Placer County. Season passes and lessons available. World-class resort that hosted the 1960 Winter Olympics.
Wine & craft beverage

Wine country is closer
than you think.

Napa Valley is about 90 minutes from Rocklin. But you do not need to drive that far — Amador County wine country is just one hour away, and Folsom and Auburn have excellent local craft breweries that have become community gathering spots for Placer County residents.

🍷
Amador County Wine Country
Plymouth, CA · ~1 hour from Rocklin · Shenandoah Valley AVA with 40+ wineries. Zinfandel, Barbera, and Syrah specialties. A favorite weekend destination for Placer County families.
🍺
Moonraker Brewing — Auburn
12970 Earhart Ave Ste 100, Auburn, CA · (530) 745-6816 · moonrakerbrewing.com · Award-winning craft brewery in the Sierra foothills. Taproom with food trucks, outdoor seating, and live events.
🍺
Red Bus Brewing — Folsom
802 Reading St Unit A, Folsom, CA · (916) 467-7790 · redbusbrew.com · Popular local brewery in historic Folsom. Rotating taps, family-friendly taproom, and regular community events.
